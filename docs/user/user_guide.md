# QuantumTrade User Guide

Welcome to QuantumTrade, an AI-driven trading platform that helps you make informed trading decisions through advanced algorithms and real-time market analysis.

## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. Visit the QuantumTrade platform at [your-domain.com](http://your-domain.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
3. Enter your email and create a secure password
4. Verify your email address through the confirmation email
5. Log in to your account

### Initial Configuration

After logging in, you'll be guided through the initial setup:

1. Connect your trading accounts (optional)
2. Set your risk preferences
3. Select your preferred trading strategies
4. Configure notification settings

## Dashboard Overview

### Main Dashboard

The main dashboard provides an overview of your trading activities:

-   **Portfolio Value**: Current value of your portfolio
-   **Today's PnL**: Profit and loss for the current day
-   **Active Positions**: Open trading positions
-   **Market Overview**: Key market indicators

### Navigation Menu

-   **Dashboard**: Main overview page
-   **Portfolio**: Detailed portfolio management
-   **Trading**: Execute trades and view positions
-   **Strategies**: Configure and manage trading strategies
-   **Analytics**: Performance reports and analysis
-   **Settings**: Account and system settings

## Portfolio Management

### Viewing Your Portfolio

Navigate to the Portfolio section to see:

-   Current positions with real-time prices
-   Profit/Loss for each position
-   Allocation by asset class
-   Historical performance charts

### Adding Positions

To manually add a position:

1. Click "Add Position" in the Portfolio section
2. Select the asset (e.g., BTC-USD, AAPL)
3. Enter the quantity and average purchase price
4. Click "Save"

### Removing Positions

To close a position:

1. Find the position in your portfolio
2. Click the "Close" button
3. Confirm the action

## Trading Operations

### Market Analysis

Before trading, review the market analysis:

1. **Technical Indicators**: Moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands
2. **Market Sentiment**: Bullish/bearish indicators
3. **News Feed**: Relevant market news
4. **Economic Calendar**: Upcoming events

### Executing Trades

To execute a trade:

1. Navigate to the Trading section
2. Select the asset you want to trade
3. Choose the action (Buy/Sell)
4. Enter the quantity or dollar amount
5. Review the order details
6. Click "Execute Trade"

### Order Types

-   **Market Order**: Execute immediately at current market price
-   **Limit Order**: Execute at a specific price or better
-   **Stop Order**: Execute when price reaches a specific level

### Trade History

View your trade history in the Trading section:

-   Filter by date range
-   Sort by asset or profit/loss
-   Export to CSV for record keeping

## Strategy Management

### Available Strategies

QuantumTrade offers several built-in strategies:

-   **Moving Average**: Trades based on moving average crossovers
-   **RSI**: Uses Relative Strength Index for overbought/oversided signals
-   **MACD**: Moving Average Convergence Divergence strategy
-   **Bollinger Bands**: Trades based on volatility bands

### Configuring Strategies

To configure a strategy:

1. Go to the Strategies section
2. Select the strategy you want to configure
3. Adjust parameters (periods, thresholds, etc.)
4. Set the confidence level
5. Enable or disable the strategy

### Strategy Performance

Monitor strategy performance:

-   Win rate and profitability
-   Risk-adjusted returns
-   Drawdown analysis
-   Comparison with buy-and-hold
